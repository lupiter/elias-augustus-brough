### Expanded


Luke 2:7 "Laid in a manger because there was no room
in the inn."

Xmas day.

Xmas greetings from Australia. Cards etc. Gospel tracts and chocolate. Visited by Captain Brent, Sister Gallin and Sister Kenedy. Wonder what kind of time they are having at home also at Garden Vale. Guess they are not forgetful of me. Writing to Clare Stan to have letter posted privately.

I first sighted land about 5 pm. Anchored 7 p m. Plymouth Sound. How much I have read about this place! It's historical value.

Meals not as good as usual. Dry fowl and rice pudding.

Nearly all boys came up to see me.
