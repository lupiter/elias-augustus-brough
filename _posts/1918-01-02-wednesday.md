### Expanded

Psalm 25:4 "Show me thy ways, O Lord; teach me thy paths."

Got up at 7 a.m. feeling a bit shaky.

Good breakfast. Expected to go out with S Mjr + the other boys for a walk. Was considered too weak so had to stay at home. Boys enjoyed trip. Say they saw Dartmoor. Was a bit disappointed not being able to go.

Had good chat with the boys during evening also Sister included Gospel 'Second coming' Jewish restoration. Man's personal responsibility. Many were interested. L/Cpr H very much so. Expect to hear more of him.

Teach me thy path O Lord and give me grace to walk therein.

Helped sister make some rock cakes for supper but missed my soup in doing so.
