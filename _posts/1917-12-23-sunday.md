### Transcription

    Heb 1-6
        "I will be to him a Father
        and he shall be to me a son"

    Woke up feeling unwell.
    Boys said my face was
    swoollen. Went on sick
    parade. Capt. Brend thought
    mumps. Off Duty
    Major said not to Isolat
    till later.

    P.M. Getting worse. Capt Brent
    arrange for me to be with
    Cpl Necoman in top Isolat.
    This was very good of him.
    Sid Burton kindly brought up
    my kits + packed them.
    Nice bed, clean + comfy.
    Slept all afternoon.
    Very bad cold. No pain.


### Expanded

Hebrews 1:6 "I will be to him a Father and he shall be to me a son"

Woke up feeling unwell. Boys said my face was swollen. Went on sick parade. Capt. Brend thought mumps. Off Duty

Major said not to Isolate till later.

P.M. Getting worse. Captain Brent arranged for me to be with Corporal Necoman in top Isolation. This was very good of him. Sid Burton kindly brought up my kits and packed them. 

Nice bed, clean and comfy. 

Slept all afternoon.

Very bad cold. No pain.
