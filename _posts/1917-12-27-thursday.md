### Expanded

Mark 14:27 "I will smite the Shepherd + the sheep will be scattered."

Awakened about 7am. Hot water to wash in. Had a fair night. Bleeding at the nose. lost lot of blood
feel better for it.

Visited by a Captain also Dr. in civil clothes. Night orderly a Jew. Abraham by name. "a bird" Has
to keep two fires burning. Work too strenuous. Fell asleep.

Australian Red Cross supplied writing material tobacco etc. Say they will provide more stuff. Ralph his throat a little better. In bed all day. 

Sun shining beautifully. Evenings cold.
