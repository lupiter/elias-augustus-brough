### Expanded

Ecclesiastes 9:10 "Whatsoever they hand findeth to do, do it with thy might"

The beginning of the New Year was begun by a very bad ear ache. Gave trouble through the night. Had linseed poultices.

Nurse kindly placed a nice rosy apple on each locker. These were greatly appreciated.

Visit by Matron ?? also Chaplain. Later did not stay.

Wrote to my little girlie. (No 4) also am posting letters to Mater, Mr Adams + Mr Booksay. 

Finished reading "Laddie" also had a read in Song of Songs. Asked if I could get up this evening. Surprised "Yes, for a little while".
