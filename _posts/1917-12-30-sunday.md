### Transcription

    II Cor 13-8
    "We can do nothing _against_ the
    truth but _for_ thre truth"
    Still washed in bed. Must get up in
    future. Went round ward + gave
    out the remainder of tracts I had
    with me. Many read them. Trust the
    Lord will use His Word.
    Have been running through
    Books of the Bible. A Memory
    Refresher. Had good breakfast. also
    good night. How _good_ is the God
    we adore! I / Cpl H seems to have good
    knowledge of Scripture. R- reads
    regularly but I don't think he's comitted.
    N- evidently has a praying uncle +
    a neice in Missionary field. Sister
    has evangelistical sympathies.
    May the Word of the Lord abound
    in the hearts of men + women that
    they may honour Him whom to know is
    "life Eternal"

### Expanded

2 Corithians 13:8 "We can do nothing _against_ the truth but _for_ thre truth"

Still washed in bed. Must get up in future. Went round ward and gave out the remainder of tracts I had with me. Many read them. Trust the Lord will use His Word.

Have been running through Books of the Bible. A Memory Refresher. Had good breakfast, also
good night. How _good_ is the God we adore! I / Corporal H seems to have good knowledge of Scripture. R- reads regularly but I don't think he's comitted. N- evidently has a praying uncle +
a neice in Missionary field. Sister has evangelistical sympathies.

May the Word of the Lord abound in the hearts of men + women that they may honour Him whom to know is "life Eternal"
