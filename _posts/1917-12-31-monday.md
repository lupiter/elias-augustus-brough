### Expanded

Song of Songs 2:3 "I sat down under his shadow with great delight and his fruit was sweet to my taste"

During the night left side of face became swollen. Had to stay in bed.

Last day of old year. Nothing exciting excepting a few songs from the boys in the evening and a general stampede and roar when Abraham came on duty. Night sister allowed me up to sit in basket chair beside the fire.

Night cold. Sister very nice, came and talked to each. Spent most time at Ralphs bed and mine.
